# Code of Conduct

This repository is for practical, evidence-aware collaboration around MCP workflow patterns. Discussion should stay specific, respectful, and useful for contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and language fluency.

## Expected Behavior

- Critique claims, sources, wording, and workflow design without attacking people.
- Keep disagreements concrete: point to the sentence, label, or assumption that should change.
- Be honest about uncertainty, evidence quality, and safety limits.
- Respect contributors who are writing in English or Chinese as a second language.
- Make room for questions, corrections, and beginner contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal insults.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Doxxing, stalking, or sharing someone else's private information without consent.
- Repeated bad-faith arguing, trolling, dogpiling, or derailing.
- Knowingly posting fabricated evidence, malicious links, or unsafe instructions.
- Using issues or pull requests to shame contributors for mistakes or language limitations.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and any repository-linked spaces where someone is participating as part of this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, contact the maintainers privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profiles. If private contact is not available, open a minimal public issue asking a maintainer to reach out and do not include sensitive details.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, ask for changes, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and workable. They should act proportionally and explain moderation decisions when it is safe to do so.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or helps with moderation is not acceptable.
